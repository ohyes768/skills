# F10 unified financial MVP

Use F10 consolidated three statements plus main financial indicators as the explicit financial source. Do not send the agent to verify individual numbers online. Preserve original stock identity/input, ten-field output, missing values and no-PDF boundary. Suspend bank/insurance evidence collection in the new path; financial firms receive a limited profitability assessment without industrial leverage penalties or claims about regulatory safety.

Acceptance: one bounded fetch command archives four datasets and provenance; deterministic normalization and scoring; finalizer verifies the packet against original identity and recomputes E; legacy commands remain compatible; offline regressions cover periods, missing/duplicate rows, financial sectors and finalizer drift. No NAS deployment.
